Target Users

Residents of a rural or peri-urban community with limited access to safely managed drinking water, unreliable food production, and few local paid opportunities. The pilot is designed around local agreement (illustrative size ~40 households).

User Persona — Fatima

Age: 34  |  Role: Parent and small-scale grower

Fatima lives with her two children. She spends significant time collecting water from a distant or unreliable source. Harvests vary with the season and water availability. Spare cash is limited and illness from unsafe water is a real risk.

Goals: Safer water closer to home, more reliable vegetables for the family, and possibly a small amount of income from surplus produce or a maintenance role.

Frustrations: Time lost collecting water, uncertain water quality, variable harvests, and few nearby paid opportunities.

Simple User Journey

  1. Today: Parent spends time getting water. Vegetable harvests vary. Spare cash is limited.
  2. With the hub: Water collection becomes easier at a maintained station. A shared garden may provide food or small sales. Training opens a path to paid operator or repair work.
  3. What to verify: Did travel time, food availability, or income actually change? Track outcomes including for vulnerable households.
