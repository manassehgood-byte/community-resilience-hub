How the Hub Works

A modular, off-grid design operated with the community. Three linked services:

1. Safer Drinking Water

  1. Test the source for microbial and chemical risks
  2. Treat and store with a validated process and protected storage
  3. Verify safety with repeated tests and maintenance logs

Solar pump + treatment + safe storage + tap. Separate irrigation water never enters the drinking line.

2. More Reliable Local Food

  • Small shared growing plot
  • Drip irrigation from a sustainable, approved non-potable source
  • Locally suitable crops and training

Rules: never divert drinking water; local growers choose priorities.

3. Local Work & Simple Monitoring

  • Tank level, flow rate and solar status shown on a local display
  • Optional SMS alerts to the operator
  • Training for paid operation or repairs

Offline-first. No smartphone or digital ID required to access water.

What Residents Use

  • Water point — fill containers at a maintained station
  • Growing area — join a shared growing programme
  • Local role — train for paid operation or repairs
