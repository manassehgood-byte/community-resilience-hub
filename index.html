Project Title

Community Resilience Hub

A locally managed hub that delivers safer water, supports small-scale food growing, and creates a path to paid work.

Problem Statement

Extreme poverty, chronic hunger and lack of safely managed drinking water are connected challenges. Around 811 million people live in extreme poverty, 645 million face chronic hunger, and 2.1 billion lack safely managed drinking water.

Families often face unsafe or expensive water, lost time collecting supplies, illness, and uncertain food and income at the same time. Single-focus projects (water-only, food-only or cash-only) leave other pressures unaddressed.

Our Proposal

We propose a modular, off-grid Community Resilience Hub operated with the community. It links three services:

  • Safer drinking water (test, treat, store, monitor)
  • Shared small-scale food growing with drip irrigation
  • Local paid roles for operation and maintenance

The hub complements existing public services. It starts with a small pilot (illustrative size: ~40 households), co-designed with the community, and measures real outcomes before any expansion.
