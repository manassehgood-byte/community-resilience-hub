UN Sustainable Development Goals

This project addresses three connected SDGs.

SDG 1 — No Poverty

Families need stable livelihoods. The hub reduces time and health burdens from water collection and illness, supports local paid maintenance work, and can improve resilience to shocks.

SDG 2 — Zero Hunger

Families need sufficient nutritious food. A shared growing plot with drip irrigation and training supports small-scale production for own use or small sales.

SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation

Families need safe, reliable water. The hub focuses on source testing, appropriate treatment, protected storage, monitoring and community-managed access.

Why one integrated hub?

  • Water-only projects improve access but may not create income
  • Food-only projects depend on reliable water
  • Cash-only support helps purchasing power but services may still fail

One service hub planned around local needs can interrupt several links in the poverty cycle at the same time.
